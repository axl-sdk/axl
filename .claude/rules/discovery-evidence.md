# Discovery evidence

<!-- No paths frontmatter: load unconditionally. Applies whenever read-only
     discovery feeds a plan, acceptance criterion, review verdict, or decision. -->

Discovery locates source and consumers; it does not decide architecture, scope,
or effort. Ask conceptual questions ("where is usage aggregated into cost?")
rather than anchoring the search to a guessed symbol.

## Lead

- Verify a negative claim with an independent search by concept before using it
  in a plan or decision. If the report omits its search scope, treat absence as
  unverified.
- Verify a brief's symptom and the source claims a decision depends on. Read
  cited definitions and relevant callers; a README, plan status, comment, or
  confident correction is not stronger evidence than the implementation.
- Reuse applicable discovery. Seek another independent perspective when a
  consequential unresolved question warrants it, not as a routine second search.

## Discovery agent

- Label claims `found` (read at the cited path) or `inferred`; do not blend both
  in one sentence.
- Report paths/symbols, consumers, search terms and scope. Say "not found in this
  search," not "does not exist."
- Return design questions and effort estimates to the lead; finish with what you
  did not verify.

Corrections meet the same evidence bar as original claims. Agent agreement is
not proof; read a definition to its end before concluding about it.
